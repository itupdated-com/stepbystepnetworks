About Step by Step Networks

Network Setup & Infrastructure, done by people who've done it for years

Step by Step Networks handles the physical and logical layer most IT companies would rather skip: cabling, switches, access points, and the firewall rules that actually enforce your security policy.

A lot of network problems in small NYC offices trace back to a rushed initial setup: consumer-grade routers, no documentation, Wi-Fi dead zones nobody ever fixed. We build it right the first time, or fix it properly the second time.

Step by Step Networks is an IT services brand operated by HelloITGroup LLC, based in the Flatiron District. Every install is handled by the same team behind Hello IT Group's infrastructure work.
